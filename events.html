What Training Looks Like With Us

From certification courses to open community days, there is a way for everyone to get more prepared.

01

Public Courses

Certification and skills courses open to the public, from wilderness first aid to land navigation and intro rope.

02

Community Events

Preparedness days, safety clinics, and outreach built to raise readiness across the communities we serve.

03

Team Training

Ongoing operator training and joint exercises with partner agencies that keep our team mission ready.
