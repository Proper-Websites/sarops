SAR OPS is a non-profit volunteer search and rescue organization specializing in wilderness, urban, technical, and disaster-response operations. Our team is trained in rope rescue, high-angle and low-angle rescue, swiftwater rescue, wilderness search, remote patient care, and complex terrain operations. We stand ready to assist agencies and communities when lives are at risk and every minute matters.

What We Do

Built for Every Terrain

Ground Search & Rescue
Coordinated search operations for missing and overdue persons in urban, rural, and wilderness environments, including lost hikers, vulnerable adults, missing children, and disaster-related searches.
Technical Rope Rescue
Specialized rope rescue operations utilizing high-angle, low-angle, and technical access systems in vertical, remote, industrial, and difficult-to-access environments.
Wilderness Search & Rescue
Search, navigation, patient access, and remote-area rescue operations in mountains, deserts, forests, and other wilderness environments.
Swift Water Rescue
Rescue and recovery operations in moving water environments, including flood incidents, river emergencies, low-water crossings, and water-adjacent terrain.
Training & Certification
Ongoing professional development, technical rescue training, and emergency preparedness programs designed to build capable responders and resilient communities.
Community & Agency Support
Supporting public safety agencies, emergency managers, schools, and community organizations through training, preparedness initiatives, and operational collaboration.
Disaster Response & Emergency Support
Supporting communities and partner agencies during disasters through emergency operations, damage assessment, logistical support, communications, and field response capabilities.
What Guides Us

Six principles, every call

The standards we hold ourselves to before, during, and after every operation we run.

01
Integrity

We do what is right on every call, whether or not anyone is watching.

02
Service

We answer because someone needs us, not for recognition.

03
Excellence

We train to meet or exceed national standards, and then keep training.

04
Teamwork

No operator works alone. Every rescue is a coordinated effort.

05
Resilience

Hard terrain, long hours, bad weather. We finish what we start.

06
Compassion

Every person we reach is treated with dignity and care.

Our Mission

Trained to respond, built to rescue

SAR OPS was built on a foundation of service, professionalism, and community. Our volunteer responders train in technical rescue, wilderness operations, emergency medical care, swiftwater rescue, search operations, and incident management to safely operate in challenging environments.

Every mission represents a person, a family, and a community in need. We respond with professionalism, compassion, and a commitment to excellence.
