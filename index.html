What We Do

Built for Every Terrain

Ground Search & Rescue
Coordinated ground operations for missing persons in urban and rural environments: lost hikers, elderly with dementia, missing children.
→
Technical Rope Rescue
Vertical, precision-driven rescue from cliffs, ravines, bridges, and other technical environments where traditional methods will not work.
→
Wilderness Search & Rescue
Advanced survival, tracking, and terrain navigation for individuals lost in forests, deserts, mountains, and remote backcountry.
→
Swift Water Rescue
Specialized response in moving water (flood events, river incidents, and water-adjacent terrain) by certified swift water technicians.
→
Training & Certification
Internal and community-based training that meets or exceeds national standards, because preparedness saves lives.
→
Community & Agency Support
Partnerships with communities, schools, and agencies to strengthen emergency preparedness across the region we serve.
→
What Guides Us

Six principles, every call

The standards we hold ourselves to before, during, and after every operation we run.

01
Integrity

We do what is right on every call, whether or not anyone is watching.

02
Service

We answer because someone needs us, not for recognition.

03
Excellence

We train to meet or exceed national standards, and then keep training.

04
Teamwork

No operator works alone. Every rescue is a coordinated effort.

05
Resilience

Hard terrain, long hours, bad weather. We finish what we start.

06
Compassion

Every person we reach is treated with dignity and care.

Our Mission

Trained to respond, built to rescue

SAR OPS was built on a foundation of service, courage, and community. Our 100% volunteer team is certified in technical rope rescue, swift water rescue, wilderness first response, EMS response, and urban SAR.

Every call we answer is a life that matters, and we respond with urgency, empathy, and elite training.
