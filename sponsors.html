Our Sponsors

Standing with us in the field

Why Sponsorship Matters

Funding the missions that bring people home

Equipment must be operational. Volunteers must be trained. Response capabilities must already be in place.

Sponsorship makes that readiness possible. Every contribution directly supports the equipment, training, and operational capability required to respond before an emergency ever occurs.

How Sponsors Make A Difference

Where your support goes

Sponsorship directly funds the four things every rescue depends on, long before the page ever comes through.

Equipment Maintenance & Upgrades

Ropes, hardware, radios, and rescue systems inspected, replaced, and upgraded to standard.

Volunteer Training & Safety Gear

Certification courses, personal protective equipment, and the gear that keeps operators safe.

Mission Readiness & Logistics

Fuel, vehicle upkeep, and the operational logistics that get a team to the scene fast.

Community Outreach & Preparedness

Public education, preparedness events, and joint exercises that strengthen the whole region.

Partners In Readiness

This is where the magic happens

The operational manufacturers behind our gear, communications, and rigging. Their support keeps SAR OPS equipped to the standard every rescue demands.

Technical Rescue Equipment Partner

Petzl

Donated rescue equipment and ongoing affiliate support, outfitting our team with the technical gear that vertical rescue depends on.
Communications Partner

Motorola Solutions

Programming support, dealer access, camera integration support, and mission-critical communications assistance that keeps our team connected in the field.

Vehicle Fire Suppression Partner

H3R Performance

Clean-agent fire extinguishers mounted across our response fleet, protecting crews and apparatus on every callout.
Technical Hardware Partner

Rock Exotica

Discounted support pricing for rescue hardware and rigging equipment, keeping our rope systems built on hardware trusted at the edge.
Rope Systems Partner

Sterling

Discounted access to rescue ropes and technical equipment, the lifeline at the core of every technical rope evolution we run.
Emergency Lighting Partner

Whelen Engineering

Whelen Engineering provides the emergency lighting and control system for SAR OPS' primary response vehicle, improving responder safety, scene visibility, and operational readiness during emergency deployments.
